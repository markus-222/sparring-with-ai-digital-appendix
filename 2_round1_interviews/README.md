# Round 1 Interviews

Material of the ten expert interviews described in Sections 3.2.2 and 4.2 and Appendices B and C of the thesis. The documents are the German working originals; the interview guide is printed in Appendix C.

| File or folder | Content |
|---|---|
| [`codebook.md`](codebook.md) | Complete codebook: general coding rules and the deductive (D1–D17) and inductive (I1–I13) categories with definitions, anchor examples, and coding rules |
| [`coded_segments.xlsx`](coded_segments.xlsx) | Coded segments of all interviews, one row per segment and category with interview, time stamp, category, and type (phenomenon, countermeasure, or checked non-instance), and a matrix of coded segments per category and interview |
| [`transcripts/`](transcripts/) | Anonymized transcript of each interview (`P1.txt`–`P10.txt`); `I` is the interviewer, `B1` the interviewee |
