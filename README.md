# Sparring with AI – Digital Supplementary Material

Digital supplementary material (Appendix E) of the master's thesis *Sparring with AI: A Multi-Agent System for Better Requirements Inquiry* by Markus Schneele, Leipzig University, Faculty of Economics and Management Science, 2026.

The folders follow the order of the thesis.

| Folder | Content |
|---|---|
| [`1_literature_review/`](1_literature_review/) | Search strings and screening criteria and prompt of the literature review (Section 3.2.1, Appendix A) |
| [`2_round1_interviews/`](2_round1_interviews/) | Complete codebook with definitions, anchor examples, and coding rules, coded segments of all interviews, and the transcript of each interview (Sections 3.2.2 and 4.2, Appendices B and C) |
| [`3_artifact/`](3_artifact/) | Source code of the prototype in the version of Round 2, the instructions of the three agent roles, and the structure files of the three work products (Chapter 5) |
| [`4_round2_evaluation/`](4_round2_evaluation/) | Task sheets, client documents, rubric with all reference elements, codebook, coded segments of all sessions, and per session the transcript, work products, and scoring sheet (Chapter 6, Appendix D) |

## Participant IDs

Participants appear as P1 to P15, as in Appendix B of the thesis. The material of Round 2 is named by these IDs: P1, P4, P7, P8, and P10 worked in the artifact condition, which the German working documents call *Treatment*, and P3 and P11 to P14 in the baseline condition.

## Anonymization

In the transcripts, names of people, the employer, clients, projects, and places are replaced by placeholders in square brackets, such as `[Arbeitgeber]`, `[Kunde A]`, or `[Name]`. The persons, companies, and places in the Round 2 material other than the participants belong to the fictional client case.

## Language

Most documents are the German working originals. The thesis quotes them in the author's translation.

## Not Included

Full texts of the reviewed papers (copyright) and the raw exports of the database searches.
