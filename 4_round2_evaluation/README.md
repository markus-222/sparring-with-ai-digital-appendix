# Round 2 Evaluation

Material of the comparative study described in Chapter 6 and Appendix D of the thesis. The documents are the German working originals; they call the artifact condition *Treatment*. Files and folders are named by the participant IDs of the thesis.

| File or folder | Content |
|---|---|
| [`task_sheets/`](task_sheets/) | Task sheets of both conditions (`task_sheet_artifact.pdf`, `task_sheet_baseline.pdf`), which differ only in the tool to be used; Appendix D gives the English translation |
| [`client_documents/`](client_documents/) | The three documents of the fictional client as PDF: project mandate, minutes of the meeting of the division heads, and excerpt from the employee survey. The work products cite them by their German titles (Projektauftrag Lernoffensive 2027, Protokoll BL-Runde, Mitarbeiterbefragung 2026) |
| [`rubric.md`](rubric.md) | Rubric for the work products: scoring rules and calibration, scenario with its actual problem and chain of causes, task, measurement rules with anchor examples, and dimensions R1–R8 with all reference elements and hit criteria |
| [`codebook.md`](codebook.md) | Complete codebook: main categories N0 and D1–D13, subcategories I1–I17, coding rules, and the assignment of the guide questions, numbered as in Appendix D |
| [`coded_segments.xlsx`](coded_segments.xlsx) | Coded segments of all sessions, one row per segment and category with location in the transcript, guide question, direction for D1–D6, N0 level, tool element, and notes, and a matrix of coded segments per category and session |
| [`transcripts/`](transcripts/) | Anonymized transcript of each session (`P1.txt`–`P14.txt`); `I` is the interviewer, `B1` the interviewee. The 30-minute task was not recorded |
| [`scoring/`](scoring/) | Scoring sheet of each session (`P1.md`–`P14.md`), the key figures computed from them (`key_figures.md`), and the procedure with the interpretations decided during scoring (`README.md`) |
| [`work_products/`](work_products/) | The three work products of each session as saved at the end of the task; P13 also created two diagrams, included as images with a transcription |

In the scoring sheets of P3, P11, and P13, codes such as `B1` or `B5` after a line reference or at the start of a question row are the participants' own IDs for questions or sections of their work products.
